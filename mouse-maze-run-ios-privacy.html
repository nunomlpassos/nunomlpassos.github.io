Mouse Maze Run for iOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Mouse Maze Run for iOS is operated by Nuno Passos (Barnun Games). For support or privacy requests, contact mousemazerun@gmail.com. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app.

Game data and purchases

  • Progress and settings: the app stores your progress, inventory and preferences on your device. When the connected store is enabled and online, it synchronizes game data needed to deliver purchases and Maze Pass rewards with our Supabase server. Restricted developer tools can show the latest synchronized progress, balances and purchase status by Player ID. We do not record a history of each move in a maze.
  • Anonymous identity: the connected app creates a player account and installation identifier without asking you for a name or email. These identifiers link saved game data, purchase records and reward delivery. An anonymous account is still an identifier; this does not mean that no data is collected.
  • Apple purchases: Apple handles the purchase and payment process. We receive signed transaction information, product and transaction identifiers, purchase environment, purchase or revocation dates, and reward delivery records. We use these to verify purchases, avoid duplicate rewards and process refunds or revocations. We do not receive your Apple Account password, payment-card details or banking credentials. TestFlight test transactions are kept separate from production transactions.
  • Support: if you contact us, we receive your email and the message, Player ID or diagnostic details you choose to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary private information.
  • Reminders: optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device after iOS permission is granted. These reminders do not require a remote push notification token.

Advertising

The app integrates Google AdMob. A labelled banner may appear below the controls during maze levels. Rewarded ads are optional. Closing an ad early does not remove existing progress; a reward is granted only when the advertising SDK confirms completion.

All players receive child-directed ad treatment, a maximum ad content rating of G and disabled ad personalization. We do not ask for your age. AdMob publisher first-party identifiers and SDK coarse-location collection are disabled. The app does not request location permission or App Tracking Transparency permission to access the advertising identifier. The applicable Google privacy checks must allow ads before requests are made.

Advertising still involves data processing. Google may process network information such as your IP address, app or device identifiers, advertising impressions and interactions, and performance or diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, maintain its service and prevent fraud. Our configuration disables the SDK's crash reporting and coarse-location collection. These settings do not prevent the network connection needed to request an ad or imply that the SDK collects no other data.

See How Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's Privacy Policy. When Google requires advertising privacy choices, the app makes them available in Settings.

Purposes and service providers

We use game and purchase data to provide the game, deliver purchased items, synchronize rewards, understand players' latest progress, answer support requests, protect accounts, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Depending on the activity, the basis is providing the requested service, our legitimate interest in operating and protecting the game, a legal obligation, or consent where required.

We use Supabase for account and game data hosting, Apple for distribution and purchases, and Google AdMob for ads. Providers may process information outside your country under their privacy terms and applicable safeguards. We do not sell player data.

Access, correction and deletion

You or a parent or guardian can request access, correction or deletion by emailing mousemazerun@gmail.com. If available, include the Player ID from Settings > Help & Support so we can find the anonymous account. You can also make a request after uninstalling. We may need to verify ownership before changing or deleting account data.

Server records are kept while needed to operate the account, deliver purchases, resolve disputes or refunds and meet legal obligations. On an accepted deletion request, we remove account and game data except for limited records that must be retained for legal or fraud-prevention reasons. Deleting server data does not automatically erase a local save or a separate device backup. Contact us before deleting the app if you need help protecting your progress.

You can also raise a privacy concern with your local data protection authority; in Portugal this is the CNPD.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest date appears above. Visit the iOS support page or email us if you have questions.
