Mouse Maze Run for iOS — Support

Need help with Mouse Maze Run on your iPhone or iPad? Email mousemazerun@gmail.com.

Developed by Nuno Passos (Barnun Games).

Before contacting us

In the game, open Settings > Help & Support. If possible, include your Player ID, game version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Never send your Apple password, verification codes or payment-card details.

A purchase has not arrived

Connect to the internet, reopen the game and allow it to synchronize. Purchase recovery is available in Settings > Purchases. Use the Apple Account used for the purchase when Apple asks for authentication. Contact us if the item is still missing. Do not buy it again to fix a delivery problem.

Progress or rewards

Contact support before uninstalling or deleting data. Tell us your Player ID and what changed. This helps us investigate without risking your existing save.

Refunds

Apple handles requests for App Store refunds. See Apple's refund instructions. Eligibility and the final decision are determined by Apple.

Privacy and data requests

Read the iOS privacy policy. You or a parent or guardian can use the same support email to request access, correction or deletion of game data.
