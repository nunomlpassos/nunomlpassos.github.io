Mouse Maze Run — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Mouse Maze Run is operated by Nuno Passos (Barnun Games). For privacy questions or requests, email nuno.ml.passos@gmail.com.

Information used by the game

  • Progress and settings: the game stores levels, inventory, settings and a local save on your device. When the connected Play Store features are used, a copy of the game save and delivery history can be stored on our Supabase server to keep purchases and rewards consistent.
  • Player and installation identifiers: the game creates an anonymous player account and installation identifier. You do not need to provide an email address or name to play. These identifiers link your save, wallet and purchase records to your account.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes payments. We receive and store the product, purchase token, order identifier where available, transaction state, and rewards delivered so that we can verify purchases, prevent duplicate delivery and handle cancellations or refunds. We do not receive your card number or payment credentials.
  • Age choice: the game asks for a date of birth to choose an age-appropriate advertising experience. The complete date is checked on the device and is not saved or sent to our server. Only the adult or minor category is saved on the device.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive the message and any account or diagnostic details you choose to include.

Advertising and children

The game uses Google AdMob. A labeled banner may appear below the controls during maze levels. Rewarded ads are offered only to adults who choose to watch them; minors can play without them. We configure ad requests with child-directed treatment, general-audience content rating and disabled ad personalization, including for adults. The Android app does not request permission to access the advertising ID.

AdMob may process data such as IP address, approximate location inferred from it, interactions with ads and the app, diagnostics, and device or app identifiers for serving and measuring ads and preventing fraud. The advertising SDK is configured before ad requests and does not request an ad until the age choice is complete and the applicable privacy process allows it. Where required, adults can review or change advertising privacy choices in the game's Privacy settings.

For details about Google's processing, see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Why we use information

We use game and purchase information to provide the game and purchased items, synchronize rewards, answer support requests, maintain security, prevent fraud, and meet applicable legal obligations. These activities are based on providing the service you request, our legitimate interest in protecting the game and its users, or legal requirements. Where consent is required for advertising-related processing, the game requests it through Google's privacy message before requesting ads.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for account and game data hosting, Google Play for app distribution and in-app billing, and Google AdMob for advertising. These providers may process information in countries other than yours under their own privacy terms and applicable safeguards. We do not sell player data.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your Mouse Maze Run anonymous account and associated server data, email nuno.ml.passos@gmail.com. This works even if you have uninstalled the app. If the game is still installed, find your Player ID under Settings > Help & Support and include it so we can locate your anonymous account. A parent or guardian can make a request for a child. We may need to verify account ownership before deleting data. We delete the account and associated game data, except for limited purchase or dispute records that we must retain for legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

Local progress and the saved age category remain on the device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Server records are kept while needed to operate your account, deliver purchases, resolve refunds or disputes, and meet legal obligations. To request access or correction of server data, use the same email address.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The latest version and update date will appear on this page.
